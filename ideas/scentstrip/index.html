The Problem

Perfume was
never designed
for real life.

A good perfume in India costs ₹2,000–₹15,000. It runs out in weeks if you use it daily. You carry it in your bag and it breaks. You spray it in the morning and by noon it's gone.

The format hasn't changed in 100 years. The glass bottle, the pump, the liquid — it's fragile, expensive, and wasteful. There had to be a better way.

💸
Expensive to buy
₹3,000+ for a bottle most people can't afford to use freely every day. Budget fragrances compromise on quality.
⏳
Doesn't last the day
Sprayed on skin, fragrance evaporates within 4–6 hours. You're unscented by the time it matters most.
🎒
Impossible to carry
Glass bottles break. Travel-sized versions are expensive per ml. Rollerballs compromise the scent.

The Science

Every scent tells
a layered story.

Click any layer
The scent pyramid
Every fragrance — from the most expensive perfume to ScentStrip — is built on three layers of scent that unfold over time. This is what makes a fragrance complex, interesting, and personal.

ScentStrip is engineered to deliver all three layers from a strip thinner than 3mm.
Top Notes
The first impression
Light, bright molecules that hit immediately. Citrus, herbs, light florals. Gone in 30–120 minutes but they're what makes you stop someone and say "you smell amazing."
Heart Notes
The soul of the scent
The heart of the fragrance that emerges once top notes fade. Deeper florals, spices, woods. This is what you smell for most of the day — lasting 3 to 5 hours.
Base Notes
What lingers
Heavy, rich molecules — oud, sandalwood, musk, cedar, amber. These anchor the scent and can last 6–8 hours on fabric. ScentStrip's glycerin base holds and releases these slowly.

How it works

Three steps.
All day scent.

01
Peel
Lift the backing paper from your ScentStrip to expose the repositionable adhesive. Takes 2 seconds.
02
Place
Press firmly onto the inside of your collar, waistband, or inner hem. It stays exactly where you put it.
03
Wear
Your body heat gently warms the strip throughout the day, releasing fragrance in slow, natural waves.
04
Peel off
At the end of the day, peel it off cleanly. No residue. No stain. Ready for a fresh strip tomorrow.

## What is this?

**[Dhandha](https://daicikd.github.io/Dhandha/){:target="_blank"}** (ધંધા) is the Gujarati word for *business* — and that's exactly what this repo is about.

This is my public lab where every business idea I believe in gets:
- A clear problem statement
- A real landing page [built and launched]
- A waitlist to test if people actually care
- An honest result logged for the world to see

No idea stays in my head. Every idea ships.

---

## Why I built this

I'm a CS student, a builder, and someone who thinks about business constantly. I got tired of ideas dying in notes apps. So I made a rule, if I think an idea is worth pursuing, I build it, launch it, and find out if I'm right.

This repo is the paper trail of that process.

---

## The Process

Every idea here follows the same 6-step flow:

**1. Define** — Write the problem, the solution, and who it's for.

**2. Build** — A clean landing page. No over-engineering. One weekend max.

**3. Launch** — Push to GitHub Pages. Live link, real URL.

**4. Share** — One Reddit post, one LinkedIn post, one honest ask: *"Would you use this?"*

**5. Wait 72 hrs** — Let real people respond.

**6. Log the result** — Signups, feedback, and a call: pursue, park, or kill.
